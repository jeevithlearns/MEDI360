# MEDI-360: Features Overview

MEDI-360 has been beautifully streamlined to focus on core medical assistance and health management. Below is a comprehensive list of features for both our Web and Mobile platforms.

## 🌐 Web Application Features

The React-based web application provides a robust interface for comprehensive health management from your desktop or laptop.

### 1. Unified Health Dashboard
- View a consolidated summary of your health status.
- Access quick links to recent prescriptions, upcoming reminders, and overall health insights.

### 2. Comprehensive Health Profile
- Manage detailed personal medical information including age, height, weight, and blood type.
- Log ongoing medical conditions, known allergies, and current medications.
- Receive smart, personalized risk assessments based on profile data.

### 3. Medical AI Assistant (Chat)
- Interact with an advanced, Gemini-powered symptom checker.
- Receive immediate severity assessments to help determine if professional medical attention is urgently needed.
- Obtain generic health advice personalized to your existing health profile context.

### 4. Prescription Repository & OCR
- Upload photos or scanned copies of medical prescriptions.
- Automatically extract recognized medicine names, prescribed dosages, and schedules using AI OCR technology.
- Maintain a highly accessible digital history of past prescriptions.

### 5. Smart Medicine Reminders
- Schedule regular reminders for taking medications.
- View an organized itinerary of daily medicines.
- Receive web-based notifications to ensure adherence to prescribed routines.

### 6. Overall Health Analytics
- View AI-generated health metrics and insights.
- Monitor your health trend through simplified, cross-domain health reports.
- Access ML-driven predictions for long-term health risk scoring.

## 📱 Mobile Application Features

The React Native mobile app ensures your health assistant is always in your pocket, optimized for quick access and on-the-go medical checking.

### 1. Mobile Health Dashboard
- A streamlined, glanceable dashboard optimized for mobile screens.
- Swift access to the most vital daily statistics and next medication reminders.

### 2. On-the-Go Medical AI Chat
- Fast, responsive symptom checking and Q&A from anywhere.
- Voice-to-text dictation compatibility for accessible symptom description.
- Immediate localized severity and emergency detection alerting.

### 3. Quick Prescription Capture
- Utilize the device's native camera to snap a photo of a prescription instantly.
- Seamlessly process the image through the AI OCR layer to digitize doctor's instructions.
- Review and verify the digitized data directly on the mobile device.

### 4. Push Notification Reminders
- Native push notifications securely tied to your personalized medicine schedules.
- Ensures timely medicine intake regardless of whether the app is actively open.

### 5. Health Context Updates
- View and update critical aspects of the health profile directly from the app (e.g., adding a newly discovered allergy while at the clinic).
- View simplified medical insights generated by the platform.

### 6. Drug Safety & Medical Hub Integration
- Quickly cross-check medications for adverse interactions from the convenience of your phone.
- Browse personalized medical advice and safety information directly from a dedicated mobile interface.
